Frequently asked questions

Before you book your consultation.

Some common questions about legal consultations, documents and professional engagement.

What should I bring to my first consultation? −
Bring relevant documents, notices, agreements, correspondence or any records connected to your matter. A brief timeline of events can also help.
Can I consult a lawyer online? +
Online consultations may be arranged through phone or video call, subject to availability and the nature of the legal matter.
How are legal fees determined? +
Fees depend on the nature, complexity and scope of the legal work. Applicable professional rules and engagement terms will be discussed before proceeding.
Do you handle property disputes? +
Property-related consultation may include documentation, ownership issues, agreements and disputes, depending on the nature of the matter.
Does contacting you create an advocate-client relationship? +
No. Submitting an enquiry or contacting the office does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship. Formal engagement is subject to acceptance and applicable professional requirements.
